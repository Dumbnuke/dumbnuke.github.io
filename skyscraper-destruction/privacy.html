Privacy policy

Skyscraper Destruction · effective October 10, 2026

Skyscraper Destruction is a live game streamed on the YouTube channel @WreckingBallGame. This page explains what the game does with information about the people who watch and play.

What we receive

When you type in the live chat of one of our streams, the game receives what YouTube shows publicly in that chat: your display name, your YouTube channel ID and your message. It does not receive anything else about you, such as your email address.

How we use it

  • If your message is a command (like spike), the game runs it.
  • Your display name appears on the stream when you use a command, on the tag above the ball while your effect runs, and on the Top Wreckers board for that stream.
  • The game keeps a log of commands on our own computer: the time, your channel ID and display name, the command, and what happened. We use it to run the game and to see how it's played. Each day's log is deleted after 12 months.

What we share

We don't sell or share this information with anyone. Keep in mind that the stream itself is public: anything shown on it, including names on screen, can be seen by anyone watching, and YouTube keeps the recording.

YouTube API Services

Skyscraper Destruction uses YouTube API Services. We use them only with our own channel's account, to set up, start and end our broadcasts. The game never signs in to or accesses viewers' YouTube accounts.

Watching and chatting on YouTube is covered by the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy. If you ever gave this app access to a Google account, you can remove it any time at your Google account's third-party access page.

This website

This site has no cookies, ads or tracking of its own. It's hosted by GitHub Pages; see GitHub's privacy statement for what GitHub logs.

Children

The stream isn't made for children under 13.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, we'll update it here and change the date at the top. For questions, or to ask us to delete your entries from the command log, open an issue at github.com/Dumbnuke/dumbnuke.github.io/issues with your YouTube channel name (don't post anything private there).
