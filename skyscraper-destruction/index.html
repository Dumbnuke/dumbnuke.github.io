Skyscraper Destruction

A live game on YouTube: a wrecking ball drops from a crane and smashes a skyscraper floor by floor, and chat helps it along.

Watch on YouTube

How to play

Type one of these words in the stream's live chat. Anyone can play; there's nothing to sign up for.

  • explodeDrops a bomb just above the wrecking ball.
  • bounceMakes the ball bouncier and faster for 5 seconds.
  • spikeCovers the ball in spikes for 10 seconds: it breaks things three times as fast.
  • ballAdds an extra wrecking ball for 10 seconds.
  • launchRockets the ball straight down.
  • absorbThe ball swallows the rubble it touches and grows, for 20 seconds.

Each command has a short wait before the same person can use it again. Use one and your name shows up on the stream; the more your commands smash, the higher you climb on the Top Wreckers board.

Levels

Every building has a clock. Bring it down in time and the next building is taller; take too long and the level drops. Run out of time completely and it's back to level 1.
